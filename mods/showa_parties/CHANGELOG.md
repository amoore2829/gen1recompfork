# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-17

First release.

- A party every third day, rotating around the venues the rest of the suite
  registers, with three themes (TRADE MEET, BATTLE PARTY, SWAP MEET) that
  change who turns up.
- Five guests per party, drawn from the day and the room: the crowd is a pure
  function of the schedule, so the people standing there are the people the
  news said would be, and leaving the room does not reroll them.
- Three things to do: a **friendly battle** scaled to your party, an **item
  swap** ("I am after a POTION, I will give you a REVIVE"), and a **real
  POKEMON trade** performed through the engine's own trade routine.
- A host with the guest list, tonight's theme, and how many days until the
  next party. The news announces it.
- Requires `showa_core`; every other Showa mod is optional and only widens
  the venue rotation.
