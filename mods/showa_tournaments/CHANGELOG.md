# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-17

First release.

- Three cups on the National Park lawn: ROOKIE (Lv15), OPEN (Lv30, 3 badges)
  and MASTER (Lv50, 6 badges), with entry fees and prize money.
- An eight-slot single-elimination draw mixing the player, the rivals the cup
  suits, and an eight-strong house field. Seeded, so a bracket re-read is the
  same bracket.
- Your own matches are **real trainer battles**, started from the referee's
  row list through `loadtrainer` / `startbattle`. The other matches in the
  round are settled by a seeded strength roll while you play yours.
- A bracket board, per-cup win/entry records, prize money and news coverage.
- Requires `showa_core`; `showa_rivals` is optional and only changes who turns
  up.
